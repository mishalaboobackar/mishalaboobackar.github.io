G-TEC Women Power

I spearheaded G-TEC Women Power, a CSR initiative delivering free education and job placement to 2,000+ women in underserved communities. Beyond the social outcome, it strengthened brand equity and deepened regional market presence.

  • Designed and drove the program across multiple markets, from outreach to placement.
  • Connected free skills training to real job outcomes for participants.
  • Generated lasting community goodwill and authentic local testimonials.
